在项目目录PowerShell中运行。首次需要Python Pillow和imageio-ffmpeg；后者本轮装入项目`.tools/video`，未改变系统PATH。

```powershell
New-Item -ItemType Directory -Force artifacts/promo
& 'C:\Program Files\Python311\python.exe' -m pip install --target .tools/video imageio-ffmpeg
& '.tools/godot/Godot_v4.6.2-stable_win64_console.exe' --path . --resolution 1440x900 --fixed-fps 30 --disable-vsync --write-movie artifacts/promo/gameplay.avi tests/trailer_scene.tscn -- --qa
& 'C:\Program Files\Python311\python.exe' tools/render_trailer.py
# 已确认配乐生成并导出后，混入现有画面母版：
& 'C:\Program Files\Python311\python.exe' tools/render_trailer.py --music 'artifacts/promo/approved-music.mp3'
```
